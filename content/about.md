heading: Finding structure in biological variation.
affiliation: MPH in Biostatistics at Yale · Gerstein Lab · Columbia research collaboration
---
I'm interested in what variation between cells can tell us about genetic regulation. My work brings together statistics, genomics, machine learning, and cellular biology to develop methods for studying that variation.

I want to understand when a model's predictions support a biological conclusion. That means asking how its assumptions shape the result, how uncertainty enters the analysis, and what is lost when heterogeneous cells are reduced to an average.

Across single-cell genetics, RNA velocity, and computational model design, I keep returning to the same question: **what can we reliably learn from the data we have?**
