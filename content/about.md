I develop statistical and computational methods to study genetic regulation across heterogeneous cellular states. My work combines single-cell genomics with statistical modeling and simulation-based validation.

In scPME-QTL, I study how genetic effects on expression vary along continuous cellular states. In my work on Regulotype, I ask whether recurring patterns of cis-regulatory effects can help define the cellular contexts in which those effects act.

I want to connect natural genetic variation with perturbational evidence to understand **when, where, and how genetic regulation acts**.
